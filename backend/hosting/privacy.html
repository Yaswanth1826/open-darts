Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Open Darts ("the app") is made by an individual developer. This page explains what the app does with your data. Contact: opendarts.info@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Games played with the keypad, your player names and your match history stay on your phone. They need no account and are never uploaded.
  • The camera is used to find darts on your board. The pictures are processed on your phone and are never uploaded or stored.
  • An account (Google sign-in) is needed only for camera scoring. It is used to count your free camera games and to check your subscription.

What we collect, and why

DataWhy
Your Google account email address, name and a unique account ID, given to the app when you sign in with GoogleTo identify your account, so your free camera games and subscription follow you.
How many free camera games you have usedTo enforce the free trial.
Your subscription status: the end date of the paid period and the Google Play purchase tokenTo unlock camera scoring while you are subscribed, and to link a purchase to one account only.
A device identifier (Android ID). It is converted to a one-way hash on our server and the original is not keptTo stop the free trial being reset by deleting and re-creating accounts on the same phone.
An anonymous random session ID, stored on your phone and on our serverTo allow an account to be used on one device at a time: signing in on a new device signs the previous one out.
A Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check token, issued by Google, which tells us whether the request comes from the genuine app on a genuine deviceTo protect the service against fraud and abuse, such as modified copies of the app and automated free-trial farming. We receive only the pass or fail result.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera images, microphone audio or advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no advertising or analytics tools.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Google Play. We never see your payment card details. We receive only the subscription status described above.

Where data is stored and who handles it

Account data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), in Europe (Belgium and the EU multi-region). Google acts as our processor. Sign-in is provided by Google, and purchases by Google Play. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with your data.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (see below), your account, your free-game count and your subscription link are deleted from our server. The hashed device identifier and its free-game count are kept so the free trial cannot be reset; they cannot be traced back to you or to your phone's real identifier.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings, then the Account card, then "Delete my account". You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account.
  • Use the app without an account: all keypad games and your history work without signing in.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we will send you what we hold about your account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object or complain to your data-protection authority.

Games and history on your phone are removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Deleting your account does not delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for significant changes, the app will tell you.

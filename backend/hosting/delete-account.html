Open DartsPrivacy policy

Delete your Open Darts account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Open Darts and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account card, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email opendarts.info@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it and reply once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address, name and account ID.
  • Your free-game count and your subscription record.

What is kept

  • A one-way hash of your phone's device identifier with its free-game count, so the free trial cannot be reset. It cannot be traced back to you.
  • Games and history stored on your phone stay there. Clear the app's storage or uninstall to remove them.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play: open Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Open Darts, then Cancel.

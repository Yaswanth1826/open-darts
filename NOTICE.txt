DATASETS USED TO TRAIN THE DART DETECTOR

dartscribe dataset
  By Ercan Akyurek, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/geforcefan/dartscribe
  Changes: the photos were converted to object-detection labels (board corners and dart tips) and mixed with other data
  to train the detector bundled in this app.

DeepDarts dataset
  W. McNally, P. Walters, K. Vats, A. Wong and J. McPhee, "DeepDarts: Modeling Keypoints as Objects for Automatic
  Scorekeeping in Darts using a Single Camera", CVSports 2021. Dataset on IEEE DataPort:
  https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/deepdarts-dataset
